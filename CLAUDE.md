## The words an answer may be given in

The kit ships this vocabulary and no verdict spoken in it. It exists so that two hosts on two
bundles stay comparable — an answer in private words is legible to nobody but its author.

- **adopts** — this host holds the cardinal as published, whole. A complete answer, and the only one that needs no further words.
- **narrows** — this host holds less than the cardinal says, and states exactly how much less. A narrowing is a smaller claim, not a disagreement.
- **extends** — this host holds everything the cardinal says and more, and states the addition. The publisher's sentence is untouched.
- **declares-a-gap** — this host has met a question the cardinal does not answer, and says so where the answer would have been. A gap declared is a hole that leaves a trace; a gap improvised is a doctrine acquiring clauses nobody can date.
- **refuses** — this host does not hold the cardinal, and states the reason. A refusal on the record is the one form of disagreement that stays legible to the publisher and to every later reader.

## The genres a bundle may distribute

- **cardinal** — a standing verdict about conduct, stated by its publisher in no repository's vocabulary, addressed so that it can be cited rather than quoted.
- **chapter** — a body of conduct doctrine about one subject -- an economy, a risk posture, an environment -- distributed as one document and answered as one. A chapter that applies only in some environments is not gated by a condition inside itself: its bundle declares a `requires` on a profile fact, so the condition lives in the declaration and never in an interpreter.

## How this page cannot lie

- A cardinal states what its publisher holds true; a stance states what this host does with it. Two propositions, one home each -- there is nothing here to keep in step.
- Nothing on this page is edited where it landed. A distributed document is replaced wholesale by its publisher, because a document a host may edit is a fork taken silently.
- A cardinal this host has said nothing about is NAMED as unanswered. A page that showed only the answered ones would read exactly like a host that had answered everything.
- Every answer cites `bundle@semver#id` and never the prose, so two hosts that disagree produce a delta that can be printed instead of two texts that cannot be compared.
