# 15. Rule evolution -- a business rule changes by evidence, versioned
Existing rules are a design surface to improve, never a frozen legacy -- and never churned without evidence.
A change card states: the current rule and the target (a diff) · the justification (evidence, not taste) ·
impact and compatibility (who reads it, what numbers move) · the migration · the rollback · the test that
fails before and passes after · the owner. "The current rule is sufficient" is a decision too, and it is
recorded the same way.
