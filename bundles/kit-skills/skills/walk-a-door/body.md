# Walk a door

A door is a decision the kit will not make: accepting a draft, taking a capability, breaking a hold.
Your part is to make the decision legible, never to make it.

1. From the host root, run this skill's `scripts/plan.py <verb> [flags]` (the script finds
   the pinned kit and runs the verb with `--plan`). Read its output; do not re-derive it.
2. Tell the owner, in two or three sentences: what the door decides, what changes, what it costs to undo.
3. Wait for the owner's own `--approve <name>`. A name you supply yourself is not an approval.
