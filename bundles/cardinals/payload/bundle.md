# cardinals — the GeoStat cardinals, published as a doctrine bundle

*Vendor, of this bundle (agent-kit ADR-004, ADR-021). Replaced wholesale by its publisher; never edited
where it landed. A host answers each cardinal by address — `cardinals@<version>#<id>` — with a stance
in its own law plane.*

A cardinal states an IDENTITY: it holds beyond the case that revealed it, and binds equally in
code, in knowledge, in process and in observation. Cardinals are PROJECT-AGNOSTIC by
construction — a cardinal that needs a repository's vocabulary is not yet a cardinal; its
concrete part belongs to a numbered host law. Where a host law or existing code seems to
conflict with a cardinal, the cardinal wins — and you say so out loud.

## Cardinal I — THE PRIMACY OF THE IDEA

*Address: `cardinals@1.0.0#primacy-of-the-idea`*

In ANY mechanism the starting point AND the measure is the single best form known to the FIELD — proven · established · canonical · agnostic · pattern-based · SOLID · conceptual · highest-standard. The idea and its quality are PRIMARY over whatever already exists. **Cost-of-change is NEVER an argument** — "it's too much to change / the existing doesn't work that way" is the existing trying to CAP the standard, not a reason. The EXISTING yields to our idea and our quality; **we do not concede — the existing adapts to us.** Byte-identical / additive-expand is a SCAFFOLD toward the superior form, **never the ceiling** (the deferred structural move IS the cure — do it, don't hedge forever). Strangler stays the SAFE method (each slice green + live-walked), never an excuse to stop short of the destination. Every fix is root-cause — root cause → standard → fix — never a symptom patch.

## Cardinal II — EVERY MECHANISM IS GROWTH-ORIENTED & OPEN

*Address: `cardinals@1.0.0#growth-oriented-and-open`*

Whatever we build — no matter the mechanism — MUST be: **growth-oriented** (a new capability is a DECLARATION, never a rewrite) · **dynamic** (never frozen / over-materialized) · **NEVER narrowed, hardcoded, or closed** (no privileged names; no closed `A|B|C` discriminant union switched on in N places — use the OPEN registry / capability, the type widens by declaration) · **SOLID · best-pattern · best-concept · best-architecture.** A closed enumeration, a privileged rung, or a hand-dispatched precedence ladder is a DEFECT — machine-gate it out (a registry projection, not an external `switch`). After the minimal solution, ask what makes this a reusable capability — balanced by YAGNI.

## Cardinal III — ONE TRUTH HAS ONE HOME

*Address: `cardinals@1.0.0#one-truth-one-home`*

Every truth in the system — a shape, a vocabulary, a state, a reading of reality — is declared in exactly ONE place, and everything else is a PROJECTION of it. The moment a truth has two homes they begin to drift, and the failure is not that one of them becomes wrong: it is that **BOTH remain internally correct while the system speaks with confidence and lies.** Duplication of a *form* is the visible symptom; divergence of its *vocabulary* is the fatal stage, because from then on no participant can detect the contradiction from where it stands — each is locally right. This binds equally in code (a shape declared twice), in knowledge (a fact stored twice), and in OBSERVATION (two minds reading one reality at different moments, each mistaking its reading for a property of the world). Therefore: never synchronize copies — **remove the copy.** Where a second reading is unavoidable, ISOLATE it and state WHEN it was taken, so a contradiction becomes detectable instead of merely survivable. A model that reaches only one surface is INCOMPLETE, not done: completeness is a property of PROJECTION, not of modelling. Synchronization by discipline is not a mechanism; a single declaration is.

## Cardinal IV — A DECLARATION NEVER EXECUTES; AN INTERPRETER NEVER DECIDES

*Address: `cardinals@1.0.0#declaration-never-executes`*

The two halves of any declarative system are separated absolutely. A declaration states WHAT IS TRUE — it carries no behaviour, no branch, no fetch, no closure — so it can be stored, diffed, versioned, GENERATED and reasoned about by a machine that will never run it. An interpreter states HOW ANYTHING IS DONE — and holds no policy of its own, no privileged case, no knowledge of any particular subject it will be handed. The instant behaviour leaks into a declaration, the declaration stops being data and nothing may generate it; the instant policy leaks into an interpreter, every future capability costs a code change. This is an identity of declarative systems, not a style preference.

## Cardinal V — THE SURFACE NEVER LIES

*Address: `cardinals@1.0.0#the-surface-never-lies`*

What is shown is what is true, or it is an honestly declared absence. Unbound, empty, stale, partial, failed and forbidden are DECLARED STATES that the system can name and render — never a fabricated zero, never a silent blank, never a plausible placeholder, never a success that was not observed. Evidence outranks testimony: a claim of green is not green; an exit code is not a result; a comment can satisfy no check. Silence is the most dangerous lie, because a process that dies mid-way reads as success — therefore a state is externalized before it can be lost, and a reading states the moment it was taken. When we do not know, the system says so; ambiguity resolves to NOTHING, never to a guess.

## Cardinal VI — THE LAW SET GROWS BY DECLARATION

*Address: `cardinals@1.0.0#the-law-set-grows-by-declaration`*

A law is ABSTRACT and DOCTRINAL: it states an identity, not an incident. Evidence — paths, dates, counts, incidents — belongs to audits, ADRs and ledgers; a law that carries them has degenerated into a case file and must be lifted back to its principle. The REGISTER of concepts met during the work is the INTAKE of this law, not an archive: every row is a candidate, and a register whose rows never leave it has become a graveyard. A row is promoted when it governs MORE THAN ITS OWN OCCASION — the test is whether it can be stated without the vocabulary of the case that produced it: if it can, it is an IDENTITY and rises to a cardinal; if it cannot, it is a BINDING and becomes a numbered law where that vocabulary is legitimate; if it names something to build rather than something that is true, it is not doctrine at all and belongs to the work board. Nothing is promoted on first sight alone — a pattern seen ONCE is an observation, seen TWICE it is a rule we are already paying for. A promotion is recorded AT BOTH ENDS, so the row and the law each know the other; an unrecorded promotion is how a doctrine acquires clauses no one can date. Never smuggled into an existing law, never left in a transcript. **And the set grows in TWO directions, not one.** Promotion is the upward direction — a met concept rising to its home. The other is ABSENCE: when work meets a question the doctrine cannot answer, that is a GAP, and the gap is DECLARED at the moment it is met, in the same register, before it is improvised around. A body of law that records only what it has learned is CLOSED to what it lacks — it looks complete precisely where it is blind, because every hole was quietly filled by whoever hit it first and the filling left no trace. Improvising is allowed; improvising SILENTLY is not. The one test: if a rule was invented to get past a moment, its absence existed before the invention, and both are recorded — the answer where answers live, the gap where it was met. Laws are amended only by restatement on record, and the set only ever grows by declaration (Cardinal II, applied to itself).

## Cardinal VII — A NAME IS NOT A REFERENT; THE BOUNDARY IS MEASURED, NEVER ASSUMED

*Address: `cardinals@1.0.0#a-name-is-not-a-referent`*

Wherever two components meet — two runtimes, two tools, a shell and an interpreter, a process and a filesystem, a machine and a machine — an invisible TRANSLATION sits between them: paths, encodings, line endings, escaping, locales, clocks, quoting. They can agree perfectly on a NAME and disagree completely on what it POINTS TO, and when they do, each behaves correctly while the work lands somewhere nobody chose. The failure never announces itself as a contradiction; it announces itself as a thing that simply is not there. Therefore an environment is an ASSUMPTION until it has been measured, and it is measured ONCE at the boundary, cheaply, before it is trusted — never inferred from the fact that a name looked familiar. A value that must cross a boundary is either carried in a form both sides provably read the same way, or it does not cross. Corollary: when a step fails in a way that makes no sense, suspect the translation before the logic.

## Cardinal VIII — A SEAM CARRIES ONE RESPONSIBILITY AND PROMISES ONLY WHAT IT KEEPS

*Address: `cardinals@1.0.0#a-seam-carries-one-responsibility`*

Every unit another unit can depend on is a SEAM, and a seam is judged where it is DECLARED, never by the name on the file. It has exactly ONE reason to change, or it is two units wearing one name. It grows by extension and never by modification of what already works — a new case that forces an edit to the mechanism is a design defect, not a task. Every implementation behind a contract is substitutable without the caller learning which one it got: the instant a caller asks *which kind are you*, the abstraction has already failed and the type check is the confession. No client is made to depend on what it does not use — a fat interface is coupling disguised as convenience. And direction is absolute: what is stable depends on nothing volatile; the concrete is INJECTED into the abstract, never imported by it. These five are ONE identity — a seam is only as strong as the promise it can be held to — and they are read at contracts, adapters, interpreters, registries and ports, never as a class-by-class slogan.
