## What this page deliberately does not state

- **One row per document.** A file-by-file index changes on every commit, is reviewed by nobody and
  becomes the most authoritative-looking unread document in a repository. The per-file reading is
  computed at gate time, in memory, and never committed (ADR-019 decision 4).
- **Any verdict.** Nothing here is too high, too old or missing. A count becomes a finding only
  against a budget, and a budget is the host's declaration, not this page's.
- **Anything nobody declared.** A field a map row leaves out is printed as *not declared*, never as
  a plausible default, and never as a blank a reader would read as a zero.
