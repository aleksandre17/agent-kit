# Hygiene — residence, and why nothing here deletes

> VENDOR. Replaced wholesale on every install; edit it upstream or not at all.

An installed surface accumulates. Not by anyone's decision — by the ordinary act of putting a file
somewhere plausible. The failure is slow and it is never loud: a folder appears whose name describes
*how* its contents were produced rather than *what* they govern, and a year later a reader finds a
page inside it, cannot date it, cannot tell who it binds, and obeys it.

**Residence** is the rule that answers that. A residence rule names a GENRE of document, states why
that genre has no home in an installed surface, and names where it belongs instead. It is stated as
data, in one document, and the gate is an interpreter of that document. A new genre is a row; it
costs no code.

## This capability names. It does not remove.

The gate reports; the move is a human act, recorded as such. That is not caution, it is the
boundary: a deletion is a decision, a decision has an author, and no machine may be that author. A
rule that deleted would also be a rule nobody ever audits, because its evidence disappears with its
subject.

## A sensor states what it can see, before it is believed

Every genre carries two paths of its own:

- `proves` — a path the pattern **must** match. The genre's evidence that it can see the thing it
  claims to catch.
- `admits` — a lookalike the pattern **must not** match. A genre that cannot state what it tolerates
  has no measured edge, and a rule with no edge eventually names everything.

The gate checks both **before** it looks at a single file, and an uncalibrated genre is a failure in
its own right — the tree is not scanned on the word of a sensor that has not proved itself. This is
the lesson of every detector that recorded only what it was pointed at and read its own fixtures as
the world.

## What a host may do, and what it may not

A host may **add** genres, in `.agents/project/hygiene/residence.json`, under the same schema and the
same calibration duty. A host may **not** exempt a path from a genre. There is no exemption field and
there will not be one: a gate that names a defect and then permits it has already stopped being a
gate, and an exemption list is how a rule becomes a formality with a fossil record. If a rule is
wrong, it is wrong upstream, and that is where it is said.
